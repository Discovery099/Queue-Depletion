# Strategist review at generation 2,000 (end of the survey phase)

- Generations completed: 2,000
- Best certified tier: none
- Vault queries used: 0 of 60 (Vault-A), 0 of 5 (Vault-B)
- Pipeline false-discovery rate, latest placebo run: 0 Bronze, 0 Silver, 0 Gold in 1,000 placebo generations (stage 1 screen; see limits below)

Inputs read: epoch reports 1 to 4, `ledger/lessons.md`, the archive map, operator statistics, the graveyard. No vault data.

## What has been learned

1. **Nothing passes stage 1.** 2,000 minimal genomes (every trigger alone, then one trigger per pair of mechanism families with a
   context atom): 0 passes. 73% have a profit factor below 1.0 after costs. Only 5 of 1,148 genomes with at least 150 out-of-sample
   trades reach both 65% wins and a profit factor of 1.2.
2. **The real market scores above the placebo, but not by a gate-clearing margin.** Best composite score 0.42 on real data against
   0.21 on the decoy market after 1,000 placebo generations and 0.17 for the best canary genome. That is a hint of structure, not
   evidence: it is the best of 2,000 attempts.
3. **Where the hint is.** Trend-continuation entries on 15- and 30-minute bars with a structure stop: a fast/slow average cross
   (generation 781: 69% wins, twin 62%, profit factor 1.43, 355 trades on three folds) and price crossing its average with an ATR
   target (generation 1842: 74% wins, twin 55%, profit factor 1.89, but only 169 trades). Both fail the sample-size, Wilson and
   frequency gates; neither has been tested on other timeframes.
4. **Mean reversion, sweeps, exhaustion and inter-market triggers alone show no edge** after costs at stage 1 (median profit factor
   0.84 to 0.88). The standalone information test agrees: no atom's forward-return effect stands clear of what 170 null tests would
   produce (the largest t-values belong to a mix of real atoms and canaries).
5. **Win rate from geometry is everywhere.** Six of the eight highest headline win rates had alpha near zero. The twin test caught
   them. It also had a hole (level-based targets); the twin now matches target-to-stop geometry (`reports/gate_changes.md`).
6. **The binding constraints** are profit factor and win rate together, then trade count. Genomes that trade more than 200 times a
   year per instrument sit near break-even; the few with a profit factor above 1.4 trade too little.

## Directions that look exhausted for single-atom strategies

Oscillator hooks without location; candle-pattern triggers; companion-instrument triggers alone; anything with a target under one
unit of risk and no context (win rate is geometry, profit factor below 1).

## Ten hypotheses, ranked by expected information gain (queued for LLM_HYPOTHESIS generations)

1. Average cross with higher-timeframe agreement and a time stop (hardening the survey's best).
2. Price/average cross inside the owner's ATR-trail regime, ATR target, 30 minutes (does generation 1842 hold with more trades?).
3. Pullback inside a confirmed trend, bought with a resting limit order.
4. Sweep and reclaim of the prior session's extreme with the higher timeframe, exit at VWAP.
5. Initial-balance extension on expanding volume.
6. Volatility-squeeze release in the direction of the higher-timeframe average, stop to break-even.
7. Opening-range failure faded on range days.
8. VWAP band re-entry confirmed by the companion, low trend strength.
9. ES/YM divergence at a session extreme.
10. Late-session continuation in the direction of the day.

Each is written with its mechanism and falsifier in `state/hypothesis_queue.jsonl`.

## Proposed tightening of gates

Adopted: geometry-matched twins (generation 2001 onward). None further.

## What I misjudged

I expected sweep-and-reclaim triggers to show the clearest standalone edge. They did not: median alpha +1.4 points, profit factor
0.85. The survey's only lead came from the plainest trend entries on slower bars.

## Limits of this review

The placebo figure above covers the stage 1 screen only. No strategy has yet reached stages 2 to 4 on real data, so the false-discovery
rate of the later stages has not been measured.
