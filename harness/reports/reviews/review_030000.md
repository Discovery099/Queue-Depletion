## What has been learned since generation 20,000

1. **The anti-overfit changes worked.** Leaders' retention on unseen folds went from 18% (generation 20,008) to 22%, 31%, 46% and 45%
   at the four exchanges since. Home scores fell (0.57 to 0.44), which is the price of honesty.
2. **A robust but small edge exists.** A momentum thrust on 5-minute bars (generation 25968) scores the same at home and away. On all
   six test folds: 73.2% wins over 2,281 trades, random-entry twin 66.0%, profit factor 1.19, about 400 trades a year per
   instrument, every fold and both instruments profitable. The informational stage 2 run found no lookahead and no dependence on
   intrabar ordering, and no edge on decoys.
3. **Its limits are equally clear.** Profit factor 1.19 (1.11 at stress costs) against a Bronze floor of 1.40. About 2.7 of its 7.2
   alpha points also appear on no-edge decoys, so the entry skill is nearer +4.5 points. The profit disappears when each session's
   net drift is removed: it earns on trend days. Win rate falls from 73% at 5 minutes to 50% at 60 minutes, so most of the headline
   is exit geometry at that bar size. It lost money in the earliest two folds.
4. **Ensembles did not help.** Of the ten queued at 20,000, the best (price/average cross plus failed prior-day break) scored 65.8%
   wins and profit factor 1.41 on its home folds with complexity 14 and a composite score of 0.13; unions of weak entries average
   their quality, they do not add it.
5. **Home-only passes keep failing confirmation** (four so far), each for profit factor on the unseen folds.

## What I misjudged

I expected the union of low-overlap trend entries to lift frequency without lowering quality. Frequency rose; profit factor fell to
the weaker member's.

## Directions that look exhausted

Ensembles of entries whose own profit factor is 1.1 to 1.3; more parameter steps on the price/average cross.

## Ten hypotheses (queued), all aimed at the one robust candidate's weakness: payoff

Higher-timeframe agreement; ATR-trail regime; wider target with break-even; directional-path filter; tighter stop; expanding
volume; VWAP side; ensemble with the trail flip in the gap direction; limit entry on a pullback; invalidation exit at the average.
Reaching profit factor 1.4 at 73% wins needs a payoff ratio of 0.51; the candidate has 0.44.

## Honest expectation

Bronze needs profit factor 1.4; Gold needs 80% wins with 1.8. The best robust result is 73% with 1.19. Closing the Bronze gap
would take a filter that removes a third of the losers without removing winners; nothing in 30,000 generations has shown that kind
of selectivity on unseen folds.
