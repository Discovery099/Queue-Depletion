# Gate changes (Constitution Article 15: gates only tighten)

| Effective from generation | Gate | Change | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | structural.min_stop_atr | New floor: initial stop at least 0.3 ATR from the reference price | A stop inside ordinary bar noise is exit geometry, not a risk rule |
| 1 | score.sample_shrink | Composite score multiplied by min(1, trades / required trades) | A tiny sample must not lead the archive |
| 1 | stage 1 confirmation | A home-fold pass must also pass all six test folds at full thresholds before stage 2 | Performs the island exchange of SPEC 15.9 immediately instead of every 2,500 generations |
| 2001 | twin.win_rate_alpha (random-entry twin) | Twin entries are also matched to the candidate's target-to-stop distance ratio (quintile bins), not only to time of day and direction | The survey showed that level-based targets (opening-range edge, prior close) gave candidates a much shorter path to target than random bars had, producing 'alpha' of +20 points with profit factor below 1. That is exit geometry chosen by the entry, which the twin must neutralise. Generations 1 to 2000 all failed stage 1 on other gates, so no earlier verdict changes; their recorded alpha values for level-target genomes are overstated and are flagged as such in the review. |
| 15,009 | wf.in_sample_expectancy_costs (new) | The genome's record in the folds before each test fold, minus the purge, must have positive expectancy | Leaders at generation 15,008 included strategies that lost money before each home test fold and made money inside it (generation 14887: -0.93 costs per trade before, +4.39 inside). The search selects on the home folds, so a profit that exists only there is fitted. The island exchange at generation 15,008 confirmed it: profit factor 1.49 at home, 0.94 away. |
| 20,009 | score: overfit-gap factor | Symmetric: multiply by min(r, 1/r), where r is test-fold expectancy over the expectancy before the test folds; previously min(r, 1) | The search selects on its home test folds, so those are the fitted side. Island exchanges at 15,008 and 20,008 showed leaders keeping 43% and then 18% of their score on unseen folds. A record far better on the selected folds than before them now scores lower. |
| 20,009 | wf.efficiency, wf.in_sample_expectancy_costs | The record before a test fold now counts folds 1 and 2 and the island's own earlier folds only, never the other island's folds | Keeps the two islands independent, so the exchange and the all-folds confirmation remain tests on data that did not influence selection |
| 20,009 | archive admission | A genome whose trades are identical to an archived genome's (same island, timeframe, trade count, win rate, profit factor) does not enter the archive | Behavioural duplicates crowded the leader list (SPEC 9.7) |
| 20,009 | island exchange | Every 2,500 generations the 10 leading distinct strategies of each island are scored on the other island's folds; a leader keeping less than half its score there, or with profit factor at or below 1.0 there, is removed from the archive; survivors migrate | SPEC 15.9, automated |
